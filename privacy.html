Privacy

We only need to know where to send your book.

Your order

Stripe take the payment and pass us your name, address and email, so we can post the book and let you know it’s on the way. Your card details stay with Stripe — we never see them.

Nobody else sees them

We don’t sell or share your details, and there’s no mailing list to be added to. An Post see the address on the parcel, and that’s it.

Afterwards

We keep a record of the order for tax. If you’d rather your details were deleted once your book has arrived, just ask and we’ll take care of it.
