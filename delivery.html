Every signed copy is posted from Drogheda.

Getting your book

Signed copies go out within Ireland, by An Post. The €24.98 covers the book at €19.99 and the postage at €4.99.

Each book is posted within three working days. An Post usually take a day or two after that.

Changing your mind

If the book isn’t for you, just let us know within 14 days of it arriving. Email us, send it back in good condition, and we’ll refund the full €24.98. You’d only be covering the postage back.

If it arrives damaged, or doesn’t arrive at all, tell us and we’ll put it right — a new copy or your money back, postage included.

Ordering from abroad

The hardback and paperback are on Amazon and ship worldwide. If you’d like a signed copy sent abroad, no worries — email dermotgkelly@gmail.com and I’m sure we can sort something out.

Any questions

Just email dermotgkelly@gmail.com and we’ll come back to you. Stripe handle the payment, so we never see your card details.
